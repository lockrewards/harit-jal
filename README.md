# Harit Jal (हरित जल) - Custom Branded Water Bottle Platform

> **Tagline (Hindi):** *"हर बूंद में शुद्धता, आपकी अपनी पहचान"*  
> **Tagline (English):** *"Custom Branded Pure Drinking Water Exclusively For India's Finest Dining"*

A bespoke B2B bottled water e-commerce and consultation platform tailored for restaurants, dhabas, cafes, luxury banquet halls, and hotels. Every bottle is customized with the client restaurant's name (e.g., **"Karan The Dhaba"**) and supplied strictly and exclusively to that establishment.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **3D Interactive Bottle Studio**
   - Real-time Three.js 3D cylinder bottle with realistic reflections, water refraction, and custom label texture.
   - Dynamic branding: Type any restaurant name (e.g. *Karan The Dhaba*) to see the bottle update live.
   - Formats: 250ml Pocket, 500ml Classic Dine-In, 750ml Luxury Glass Decanter, 1000ml Banquet Series.
   - Cap colors: Emerald Green, Ocean Blue, Royal Gold, Midnight Black, Pearl White.
   - Table Profit & Margin Calculator for restaurant owners.

2. **Cute Small Mascot: "Neer" (नीर)**
   - Custom SVG animated water droplet mascot with blinking eyes, cheerful expressions, and an eco-leaf sprout.
   - **Interactive Web Audio Sound Effects**: Touching or clicking Neer plays water droplet "bloops", bubbly pops, and chimes without external audio dependencies.
   - Contextual guidance bubbles pointing visitors to the 3D Customizer, Certifications, and Call Booking.

3. **Round-Shaped Non-Fixed Header with Watermark**
   - Floating rounded pill/capsule navigation bar that scrolls with the page naturally.
   - Subtle Harit Jal authenticity watermark badge and responsive navigation.

4. **100% Statutory & Legal Compliance Hub**
   - **FSSAI Food Safety & Labeling**: Adherence to Packaging and Labelling Regulations 2011 and Schedule 4 GMP/GHP cleanroom bottling standards.
   - **GST Registration (GSTIN Compliant)**: Full B2B commercial tax invoicing with Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility for hospitality partners.
   - **MSME / Udyam Registration (Govt. of India)**: Verified enterprise under Ministry of MSME supporting ethical local supply chains.
   - **DPDP Act 2023 Data Privacy**: Full statutory compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, AES-256 encryption, and designated Grievance Desk.

5. **Supply Chain & Journey Roadmap**
   - 6-stage transparent supply journey:
     1. Digital Proofing with Restaurant Logo
     2. Physical Sample Verification at Dining Tables
     3. High-Speed Robotic Bottling & Nitrogen Dosing
     4. Certified Quality & Cleanroom Packaging
     5. Scheduled Direct-to-Cellar Delivery
     6. Scheduled Recurring Replenishment.

6. **Store Owner Call Booking System**
   - Comprehensive booking form for restaurant owners.
   - Captures establishment name, owner contact, monthly bottle volume, preferred date/time slot, and design notes.
   - Instant confirmation with celebration confetti, success chime, and 1-click WhatsApp quick-chat.

7. **Admin CRM, Visitor Tracking & Role Assignment Portal**
   - **Call Bookings Pipeline**: Filter, search, update lead statuses, and reassign inquiries to team members.
   - **Visitor Tracking Engine**: Tracks total visits, unique store owners, and regular repeat visitors.
   - **Regular Users Log**: Displays visit frequency, device, pages viewed, and associated email addresses.
   - **Staff & Role Management**: Add and assign new team members (Sales Manager, Operations Lead, Label Designer, Super Admin) to control leads.
   - **Google Sheets Integration & Email Alerts**: Real-time admin email dispatch logs and 1-click CSV export ready for Google Sheets master database.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 + Custom Glassmorphism & Caustic Glows
- **3D Graphics**: Three.js (Procedural Glass Shader & Canvas Label Texture)
- **Audio**: Web Audio API (Synthesized Water Droplets & Chimes)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Celebration**: Canvas-Confetti
